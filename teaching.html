02LEARNING APPROACH

Outcome → activity → assessment → reflection.

The Teaching Hub makes the relationship between intended learning, classroom activity, evidence of learning, and reflection visible without turning this public site into an LMS.

01
OUTCOME

Know the intended learning.

Each learning space begins with explicit outcomes rather than a collection of disconnected files.

02
ACTIVITY

Learn by working with ideas.

Meetings connect concepts with discussion, analysis, exercises, and practical application.

03
ASSESSMENT

Make learning observable.

Assessment is linked to intended outcomes while private submissions and grades remain in the institutional LMS.

04
REFLECTION

Turn activity into understanding.

Reflection closes the loop by connecting evidence, feedback, and the next learning step.

◉

Public learning boundary. This Teaching Hub provides public course information and learning resources. Enrolment, attendance, quizzes, submissions, grades, student data, and authenticated learning activity remain in the institutional Moodle LMS.
